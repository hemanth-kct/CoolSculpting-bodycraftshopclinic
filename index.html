FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CoolSculpting painful?

Most people experience a cooling sensation and mild pressure at the beginning of the treatment, which usually subsides within a few minutes.

How many CoolSculpting sessions will I need?

The number of sessions depends on your body goals and the treatment area. Your dermatologist will recommend a personalised treatment plan during your consultation.

When will I see the results?

Results gradually become visible over the weeks following treatment as your body naturally eliminates the treated fat cells, with full results typically seen within 2–3 months.

Who is a good candidate for CoolSculpting?

CoolSculpting is ideal for adults with stubborn fat pockets that don't respond to diet and exercise. It is not a weight-loss treatment.

Can both men & women undergo CoolSculpting?

Yes, CoolSculpting is safe and effective for both men and women looking to reduce stubborn fat and improve body contours.
